# 🍔 Food Delivery Sales Report

An interactive Power BI dashboard tracking revenue, profit margins, order status distributions, and customer purchasing patterns across geographic regions.

---


---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project provides end-to-end operational and financial performance analytics for a food delivery platform, enabling management to identify top revenue-generating locations and monitor fulfillment rates.

* **Objective:** Evaluate core financial metrics (Revenue, Profit, Discounts), order status metrics, and regional revenue distribution.
* **Tools Used:** Power BI, Microsoft Excel, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Features

* **Financial KPIs:** Tracked **$6.71M in Total Revenue**, **$2.87M in Total Profit**, and **13K items sold** across **1.323K total orders**.
* **Order Status Breakdown:** Monitored fulfillment health across **Completed (464)**, **Pending (414)**, and **Cancelled (445)** order volumes.
* **Geographic & City Insights:** Identified top-performing revenue cities (e.g., Lake Michael leading revenue) along with dynamic map mapping.
* **Customer Metrics:** Evaluated top customer records with individual state orders, total revenue generation, and customer rating scores.
* **Temporal Trends:** Monthly sales pattern analysis tracking revenue fluctuation throughout the calendar year (peaks in Feb and April).
